# BARRY SHARP'S PRO MOVER - Game Concept

## High Concept
A retro-inspired, isometric 8-bit puzzle-RPG where you play as Barry Sharp, a professional furniture mover.

## Core Pillars
1. **The "Pivot" Physics:** Moving furniture isn't just dragging; it's about momentum, angles, and narrow corners.
2. **Authentic 8-Bit Constraint:** The game must feel like it was shipped in 1998.
3. **The Gritty Grind:** RPG progression based on Barry's "Grip" (Strength) and "Hustle" (Speed) stats.

## Gameplay Loop
1. **Acquire Job:** Barry gets a call in the neighborhood.
2. **Move:** Navigate a truck grid-puzzle and then a house grid-puzzle.
3. **Deliver:** Place the furniture on target markers without breaking the client's glass vases.
4. **Upgrade:** Spend money on Barry's boots (speed) or a better moving dolly.

## Target Audience
- **Achievers:** Levelling up Barry's stats.
- **Explorers:** Navigating the open-world neighborhood.
- **Killer/Competitors:** Perfecting speed-runs of moves.
