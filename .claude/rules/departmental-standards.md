# CCGS Departmental Success & Failure Standards

## 1. ART FORGE
- **SUCCESS:** 4-color indexed PNG + .aseprite source file. Multiple of 8px resolution.
- **FAILURE:** Images with alpha transparency (not supported by GBC). Images > 160x144.
- **YAGNI:** High-res texture mockups or 3D blockouts (use minimal primitives only).

## 2. ENGINEERING BAY
- **SUCCESS:** Valid GBVM assembly wrapped in JSON UUID nodes.
- **FAILURE:** Floating point math. Duplicate UUIDs in project.gbsproj.
- **YAGNI:** Complex networking or high-level scripting abstractions.

## 3. DESIGN PIT
- **SUCCESS:** Game mechanics that resolve to 8-bit integer logic.
- **FAILURE:** Mechanics requiring > 10 active actors.
- **YAGNI:** 100-page lore docs. Keep narrative to 3-line dialogue boxes.

## 4. EXECUTIVE SUITE
- **SUCCESS:** Roman compilation without errors. Sprints completed in 7 days.
- **FAILURE:** Approving features that exceed the 192 background tile limit.
